## Allocation runs

Three runs seeded so the admin "compare runs" page has something to show:

- `run-2026-07-30-greedy-mock` (**published**) — each demo student paired with the
  better-scoring of their real rank-1/rank-2 preference (from `student-preferences.json`
  + `supervisor-preferences.json`). Labelled in the UI as a placeholder pending the
  real GA engine (task 5.0).
- `run-2026-07-29-manual` — same students, their rank-3 preference (deliberately
  weaker, as a manual baseline would be).
- `run-2026-07-28-random` — same students, their rank-5 preference (deliberately
  worst, as a random baseline would be).

This ordering (published > manual > random on mean satisfied rank and mean score)
is intentional — it's what makes the benchmark comparison demonstrate the point of
the evaluation (O6), rather than being three arbitrary equally-good runs.

`objective_score` = `((6 - rank) / 5 + supervisor_score) / 2`, rounded to 2dp — a
simple stand-in composite so every pairing has a plausible-looking number; not a
claim about what the real GA's objective function will look like.
